How applying works

About 20 minutes. Start on a computer or phone. When you need to photograph a document, you can switch to your phone and carry on.

  1. 1Choose a programmeSee the entry requirements before you choose.
  2. 2Add your National IDPhotograph it with your phone. We read the number, name and date of birth for you.
  3. 3Add your ZIMSEC resultsPhotograph your result slip. Check the grades we read, and add any resits.
  4. 4Check your eligibilityYou'll see straight away whether you meet the entry requirements, and what to do if you don't.
  5. 5Pay and submitPay the application fee by EcoCash, OneMoney, bank transfer or cash. Then track your application here and by SMS.

Have these ready

  • Your National ID, metal or plastic
  • Your ZIMSEC O-Level result slip or certificate
  • A phone that can receive SMS, with a camera
  • The application fee: US$ 20.00
