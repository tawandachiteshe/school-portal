What happens if I'm sick on a test day?

You can sit a make-up test if you give a medical note to your lecturer within 5 working days of the test.

  1. 1Get a note from a clinic or doctor with the date you were unwell.
  2. 2Hand it to the module lecturer, or to Student Affairs in Block A.
  3. 3Your lecturer sets the make-up date. It shows under Deadlines.

Without a note, a missed test counts as zero.

Source: Student Handbook 2027 · section 4.3, Missed assessments Helpful?
